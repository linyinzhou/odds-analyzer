# Counter-favorite mismatch rule

As requested on 2026-10-18, opposing fundamentals and an agreed European/Asian market favorite qualify for the counter-favorite pattern without a minimum handicap gap. Zero and reversed gaps are included. Missing markets, unknown fundamental direction, existing sample labels, Polymarket checks and staking eligibility remain separate checks.

The historical `lottery_deeper_small_win` strategy key is retained for compatibility, but its new explanatory text describes direction conflict. Historical forecasts are not rewritten. Statistics across this rule change mix different eligibility rules and should not be interpreted as validation of the revised rule.
